Original Orbital Soundscape

A theme-neutral, slowly evolving electronic soundscape using open fifths, drifting tones, spatial bell-like details, a subtle pulse, and synthesized reverberation. No third-party recordings or samples are used. The composition and synthesis code are dedicated to CC0 1.0 and may be used without royalties or attribution.

Sound is enabled by default, with a remembered explicit mute preference. Playback starts automatically when permitted, otherwise on the first interaction. It pauses in background tabs.
