Original Ambient Score

The soundtrack is synthesized locally by js/preferences.js: slow suspended harmonies and a sparse pentatonic melody, made from oscillators. It contains no third-party recordings or samples. The composition and synthesis code are dedicated to CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication), so this score may be used without royalties or attribution.

Audio starts only after the visitor enables Sound. It pauses while the browser tab is hidden. No audio files, streaming service, microphone permission, or account is required.
